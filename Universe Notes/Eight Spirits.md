The Eight Spirits are the limbs, organs, and shards of the [[Progenitor]], imprisoned while it is awake and free while it is slumbering. Each competes in the [[Stirring]]; The winner leads the awakened Progenitor; the losers lose their hold on their people

### The Great Shroom. 
Represents the School of [Abjuration] Magic. 
Manifests from the [Brain] of the Progenitor. 
Worshipped by the Myconids. 
The Great Shroom is patient, fair, lawful, precise, slow, cruel, and without mercy. 
If it loses, the Myconids lose their commanding structure and splinter into conflicting circles. 
If it wins, the Myconids become strict and mostly benevolent rulers of an order where everyone must accept their place in the system and those who refuse are met with cruelty.

### The Lusitanian Relic Urn.  
Represents the School of [Divination] Magic.  
Manifests from the [Reproductive System] of the Progenitor.  
Worshipped by the Lusitanian Settlers, who were driven to the island’s coasts with only their families and the Urn. The Urn is one parent of every Lusitanian child, birthing them from offerings of sperm or ova.  
The Lusitanian Relic Urn is shrewd, clever, cunning, gluttonous, ruthless, and hierarchical in a fatherly sense.  
If it loses, the Settlers lose access to their divination, and all but the most resourceful struggle and die.  
If it wins, the Settlers expand across the island and establish a human dynasty that will shape the world to come.

### The Yellow Eyed Raven.  
Represents the School of [Enchantment] Magic.  
Manifests from the [Eyes] of the Progenitor.  
Worshipped by those it has afflicted, generally Ogres. Afflicted beasts, particularly ravens and ogres, wander the island.  
The Yellow Eyed Raven is [not yet defined].  
If it loses, the affliction vanishes, leaving those affected with lingering side effects and scars.  
If it wins, the affliction spreads, allowing all to gaze upon the beautiful sights that the few have beheld.

### The Spirit of the Night.  
Represents the School of [Illusion] Magic.  
Manifests from the [Hair] of the Progenitor.  
Worshipped by the Werebeasts, who struggle with the inner conflict of lycanthropy.  
The Spirit of the Night is kind-hearted, forgiving, peaceful, and perhaps selfless.  
If it loses, the Werebeasts continue to struggle with lycanthropy and terrorize the island.  
If it wins, the Werebeasts are cured and all on the island live in peace, but this utopia is an illusion while the residents rot and die in reality.

### The Triad.  
Represents the School of [Necromancy] Magic.  
Manifests from the [Blood, Bone, and Skin] of the Progenitor.  
Worshipped by Vampires, Skeletons, and Zombies. Its Undead are made from its own body, so it can only grow its host by spending itself, making it the strongest Spirit with the weakest following. 
The Triad is selfish, fascistic, megalomaniacal, yet the most honest of the Eight Spirits.  
If it loses, the Undead die.  
If it wins, it kills everyone on the island and remakes them as Undead under a totalitarian order.
