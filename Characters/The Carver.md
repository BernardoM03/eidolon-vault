Old Tiefling Man

Wispy greyed beard that strings from jawline and chin, resting 2-3 inches long
Petrified rough wooden horns, mangled into sharp contorted shape, very chaotic
Dirtied and longworn robes, covered in leaves, twigs, pebbles, and caked with mud
In the palm of each hand, deeply intricate tattoos written in druidic, swirling up his arm and under his robes
- The tattoos are hidden by the rest of the robes, but swirl into the nape of his neck, where a small amber shard lies lodged within.
