The Progenitor is an alien being who found themselves on the planet by pure chance. The Progenitor is birthed of the Universe, and one could be considered a [[Spirits and Gods/Natural Spirit|Natural Spirit]]. 

Thousands of years ago, it had buried itself deep into the land to rest, before returning when the world was ready to rebirth itself anew. As it slumbered in the deep mountains, the ancient people of the land had discovered its body, and had found immense potential within its silver blood. The humans tampered with the power of what they called God within their hands, and in doing so, brought forth the strange powers. They studied its influences, knowledge, and capabilities.

The Silver Blood of The Progenitor can be referred to as [[Mercurial Magic]].
