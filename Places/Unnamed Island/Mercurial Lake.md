The Mercurial Lake is a pool made entirely of base [[Mercurial Magic]]. This pool is located deep within the underground, beyond a labyrinth of dangerous tunnels and caverns. 

This Spirit is worshipped by the [[Kuo Toa of Legsilloop]], a race of warm-blooded amphibious creatures that dwell in these caverns. They are in consistent conflict with the [[Myconids of the Great Shroom]].
