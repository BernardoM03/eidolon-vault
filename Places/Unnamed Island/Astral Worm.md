The Astral Worm is one of the [[Eight Spirits]] that occupy the shared soul of [[The Progenitor]]. It resides as the Intestines. Since landing on Dead Cradle, it has taken a strong connection to Conjuration magic.
### Astral Children
At times, the Great Worm will release batches of Astral Children from it's own form. These Astral Children are not born from a single point that develops as they age, but rather as fully grown elves or plasmoids. They only single point of origin is every Astral Child's shared memory: `An endless sea of stars and planets.` This memory is known as the `Sea of Origin`. To some they are more distant. To others, they are constant. To all, they are foundational aspects of your identity as a follower of the `Astral Worm`. 

The origin of the Astral Children is unknown to them. This topic is never addressed by the Astral Worm, but instead a concept is taught: the fate of the unknown is discovery. This has lead to different Astral Children to have different beliefs and ideas surrounding this. Some believe that the are created by and in the image of the Astral Worm. Some believe that they were swallowed by the Astral Worm, saving their people from imminent extinction. All theories and beliefs end up revolving the discussion around the Sea of Origin. 

The first Astral Children date back to roughly 200 years ago. The eldest are still alive today, serving as senior members of their community. The Island of [[Dead Cradle]] has been the only home for the Astral Children since birth. The main community lives atop the highest peak of the island, a mountainous spine along the Southern coast. Atop the peak, they have built a sacred observatory to the celestial bodies. Along different points in Dead Cradle, the Astral Children have smaller outposts and communities. These serve to discover more about the island, its secrets, and the other Great Spirits that inhabit this land.
