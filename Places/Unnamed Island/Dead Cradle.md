### Concepts and Ideas

This location will be a standalone setting that follows the rules of [[Identity]].

Identity is a cohesive narrative, pattern, or concept that defines a being. Identity can belong to objects, places, creatures, or ideas. Strong Identity attracts and condenses Magic.

One season passes each time the adventurers have an adventure

A central theme is the relationship between mortals and the gods.
- An individual worshiper may have a special relationship with a chosen deity, but acknowledges and often worships other gods.
- Mortal sacrifice is a key to a god's power, but impossible to inspire in followers without sacrifice from the god themselves. Whichever faction works best in tandem with the most important followers will achieve the greatest prize
-
Adventurers should all follow a God, enrolling themselves into what is known as Orders. These are like Cults in RuneQuest. This order cult can teach specific magic called [[Mercurial Magic]] to its members. In exchange, the member supports the cult with sacrifices, worship, and loyalty.

### Gods
The number Eight will have great significance:
[[Eight Spirits]], Eight Schools of Magic
- The [[Great Shroom]] is of Abjuration, worshipped by the Myconids
	- Patient, Fair, Lawful, Precise, Slow, Cruel, Not merciful
- The [[Astral Worm]] is of Conjuration, worshipped by the Astral Elves and Plasmoids
	- This is the faction that the players will play the first one shot in.
- The [[Lusitanian Relic Urn]] is of Divination, worshipped by the Lusitanian Settlers
	- Shrewd, Clever, Cunning, Gluttonous, Ruthless, Hierarchical in a fatherly sense
	- The Urn is always one of the parents to all children of the Lusitanians. An offering of sperm or ova is made to the Urn, who then births a child.
	- From their original home, the [[Settlers]] were driven to this island's coasts, bringing only their families and a [[Lusitanian Relic Urn]].
- The [[Yellow Eyed Raven]] is of Enchantment, worshipped by those afflicted (generally Ogres)
	- Strange beasts afflicted by the [[Yellow Eyed Raven]] wander the island, particularly ravens and ogres. 
- The [[Phoenix]] is of Evocation, worshipped by the Sun Elves and Azer
- The [[Spirit of the Night]] is of Illusion, worshipped by the [[Werebeasts]]
	- Kind-hearted, Forgiving, Peaceful, Selfless?
	- The Werebeasts struggle with the common inner-conflict of Werewolves
- The [[Triad]] is of Necromancy, worshipped by Vampires, Skeletons, and Zombies
	- Selfish, Fascistic, Most Honest of the [[Eight Spirits]], Megalomaniac
	- The Triad controls the largest share of the Progenitor’s body, Blood, Bone, and Skin, and is the most powerful Spirit in its own right. That strength does not extend to its followers. The Undead are made from the Triad’s own body rather than recruited from the living, so the Triad can only spend itself to grow its host. The other Spirits draw strength from the local peoples who worship them, multiplying their power through mortals. The Triad is the strongest spirit with the weakest following.
- The [[Mercurial Lake]] is of Transmutation, worshipped by the [[Kuo Toa of Legsilloop]]
	- No Ego, Enhances the [[Kuo Toa of Legsilloop]] and their motivations

All of these gods WANT something, but generally cannot have it

Why was there a steady state for so long. What held each of the spirits in check?
- The [[Progenitor]] sleeps and wakes at a multi-centennial scale. When the [[Progenitor]] is slumbers, the [[Eight Spirits]] that lay imprisoned within escape into the world. When the [[Progenitor]] awakes, the [[Eight Spirits]] are pulled back into imprisonment.
- As the [[Progenitor]] awakes, all of the [[Eight Spirits]] prepare for the [[Stirring]]: Generating faith, accumulating followers, raking in spiritual energy, etc... This multi-centennial [[Stirring]] determines which of the [[Eight Spirits]] wields which leads the awakened [[Progenitor]]. The champion of this event is determined by a set of rules unknown to the [[Eight Spirits]].
- The [[Eight Spirits]] cannot be destroyed. Their followers can be wiped out, as the Undead of the [[Triad]] or the Lusitanian Settlers might be, but the Spirit itself endures.
	- Because no Spirit can be eliminated, open aggression offers little lasting reward and real risk. Any Spirit that makes its move too soon marks itself as a target for the other seven. Patience is enforced by mutual suspicion.
