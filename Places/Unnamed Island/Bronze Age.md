**Labor** (skilled professions)
- **Labor I**: The settlement has one or two skilled laborers.
- **Labor II**: Most skilled professions are represented.
- **Labor III**: Every skilled profession has one or two practitioners.
- **Labor IV**: Every skilled profession has several practitioners, and one or two professions have a master.
- **Labor V**: Every skilled profession has several practitioners, including masters.

**Market** (trade)
- **Market I**: A marketplace and a few community businesses serve local needs.
- **Market II**: Large markets trade about half by barter and half by coin.
- **Market III**: Large markets and permanent shops trade by coin only.
- **Market IV**: The city is a long-distance trade hub, with merchant houses, foreign traders, and rare imported goods.

**Faith**
- **Faith I**: The settlement has a single dedicated shrine.
- **Faith II**: One temple or monastery stands alongside a few shrines.
- **Faith III**: One major temple and most popular gods have shrines.
- **Faith VI**: Every major god has a temple, including the faith’s grandest and most important ones.

**Arcane** (magic-users)
- **Arcane I**: There is a small chance of a single magic-user, usually a sage.
- **Arcane II**: There is likely a single magic-user, usually a sage or shaman.
- **Arcane III**: There are likely a few magic-users, often with apprentices.
- **Arcane IV**: About a dozen magic-users serve in the temples, a sage lives nearby, and there may be a magic shop.
- **Arcane V**: Each temple has several magic-users, and there may be a magic shop.
- **Arcane VI**: Each temple has several magic-users, and many magic shops operate.

**Society** _(suggested)_

- **Society I**: Life is organized by kinship, and decisions are made by elders.
- **Society II**: A few leading families hold more land and influence than the rest.
- **Society III**: Clear classes have formed: rulers, priests, free commoners, and laborers.
- **Society IV**: A rigid hierarchy runs from an elite of nobles and merchants down to slaves, and the poor suffer while the rich indulge.
