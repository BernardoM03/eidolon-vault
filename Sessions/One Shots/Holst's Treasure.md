### The Astral Children
`An endless sea of stars and planets.`

That is the vision that each `Astral Child` was born with. To some they are more distant. To others, they are constant. To all, they are foundational aspects of your identity as a follower of the `Astral Worm`. 

### Party Shared Backstory
You are a from a batch that was recently born, approximately 19 months and 14 days ago. That does not mean you are children, or look a similar age, but merely when you gained consciousness after the Astral Worm brought you here. That does place you relatively low on the organization structure of the Astral Children upon this island of Dead Cradle.
### The Mission
Recently, a squad of Astral Children were sent out into the Ruined Capital. Earlier than expected, a written message was sent to your base:

- `The relic is missing. Evidence points to the area being looted by the Kuo'Toa just now. We spotted a group escape through Cave L27.`

Superiors have ordered your squad to enter Cave L27, investigate the situation, and catch up these Kuo'Toa to recover the relic. 

### The Portal
At the top of the Astral Children's base of operations, there lies a sacred observatory. A large bronze disk stands in the center of the site, inlaid with the sun, moon, stars, and mapped constellations discovered by the past 200 years of Astral Children. Around the disk, Markers are set depicting the direction of the sunrise and sunset, and more.
