### Context
You are members of the [[Ordem Mundial]], a secret society in the [[State of Kin]]. An account on the [[Island of Abenasu]] mentions strange activity within the villages on the outskirts of the southern forests. You believe this to be evidence pointing to the location the fabled [[Winter Court]]. After months of politicking and convincing, you acquired the funds and resources from the [[Ordem Mundial]] for your expedition. 

You begin this adventure at a village on the outskirts of the southern forests. You have one lead; A child who mysteriously disappeared in the night about a year ago.

### Planning Notes
The missing boy's mother remembers a son but not his name. The boy's mother leaves bread and milk at the boundary stone. Each morning it's gone, replaced with a ice-frozen snow flake in its place. She begs the party to bring him home. Leads to: a night stakeout that catches a sprite collecting the offering. The sprite can be chased into the forest, but ultimately gets lost. If the party tries to track back to the village, they find that they way they were confident in does not lead back. After a day of being lost, they find a cabin in the woods.

This cabin is owned by an old man. This is the child of the village once known as Peter, but that name is long lost. The fey took his name from the village so no one could call him back, which is why everyone only knows him as the missing boy. He vanished a year ago at twelve but now looks sixty/seventy, because time ran fast for him among the fey.
- If asked about his name, he says that it should not concern them. He is just an old man in the woods. 
- If asked about the missing child, he says he found a boy dead last winter and shows the party a grave. 
- He offers to lead the party back to the village, which he can do
- He denies the existence of the Winter Court. He always dreamed of meeting the Winter Court as a child.
